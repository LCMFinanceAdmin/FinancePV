Claiming money back

Page 1 of 3

You paid for something and want it back. Anyone may do this — pastors, EXCO members, volunteers. You do not have to be on the payroll.

1
Open Submit PV.

Second item in the left-hand menu, under Dashboard. It is there for everybody.

Fill in who is paid, from which ministry, and what for.

If the money comes back to you, that is your own account — check the number twice, a wrong digit is the commonest reason a payment fails. The ministry decides whose budget it comes out of.

2
